Block Crash

Stack four. Watch them crash.

How to play

Move the top blocks of one tower onto another: tap a tower and then the tower you want, or slide your finger from one to the other. Blocks only land on the same color or in an empty tower. When four blocks of one color stand together, they crash and vanish. Empty the box in the target number of moves for three stars.

Stuck? Tap Hint to see the best next move, Undo to take a move back, or Restart. Hints are free. The lessons can be watched again in Settings › How to play.

Daily puzzle

The daily puzzle is the same box for everyone and changes at midnight UTC.

Settings

  • Language: the menus come in 70 languages.
  • Sound and Vibration can be turned off.
  • Every color also carries a symbol, so the game can be played without telling colors apart.

Contact

Found a problem? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed; start the title with "Block Crash"). Please include your device, iOS version, language and the version shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.
