Fit: Block Pattern Puzzle

Drop. Fill. Clear.

How to play

Drag one of the three pieces below onto the board. Fill a whole row or column and it clears; clears that follow each other make combos up to ×5, and a line all in one colour scores double. A pattern of cells glows gold somewhere on the board: fill every one of them for a big bonus. Gold cells on some pieces give extra points when they clear. When no piece fits anywhere, the game is over.

Each game has three Turn (a piece turns a quarter), one Hammer (one filled cell breaks) and one New pieces. Once per game you can continue after the board fills up: the middle of the board clears. The lessons can be watched again in Settings › How to play.

Daily puzzle and leaderboards

The daily puzzle gives everyone the same pieces and changes at midnight UTC. The leaderboard has three tabs: Today (the daily puzzle), All time (best endless score) and Mine (your own best games). Your name is a random one picked by the game; tap New name to get another. No account is needed.

Settings

  • Language: the menus come in 70 languages.
  • Sound and Vibration can be turned off.
  • Tiles: new colour sets unlock as you clear lines.

Contact

Found a problem? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed; start the title with "Fit"). Please include your device, iOS version, language and the version shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.
