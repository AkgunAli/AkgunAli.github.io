Plop

Drop, match and merge balls.

How to play

Slide your finger over the cup and let go: the ball drops. Two balls of the same size that touch merge into a bigger one. Merges that follow each other quickly make combos up to ×5. Keep every ball below the dashed line: if a ball rests above it for two seconds, the game is over.

Each game has one swap (the ball in your hand trades places with the next one) and one pop (tap the button, then tap a ball to remove it). The lessons can be watched again in Settings › How to play.

Daily puzzle and leaderboards

The daily puzzle gives everyone the same balls in the same order and changes at midnight UTC. The leaderboard has three tabs: Today (the daily puzzle), All time (best endless score) and Mine (your own best games). Your name is a random one picked by the game; tap New name to get another. No account is needed.

Settings

  • Language: the menus come in 70 languages.
  • Sound and Vibration can be turned off.
