Knot: Untangle Doodles

Drag. Untangle. Reveal.

How to play

Every knot is a tangle of dots and lines. Drag a dot and its lines move with it. Lines that cross are red, and a dot resting on a line that is not its own counts as a crossing too. Move the dots until nothing crosses: the dots glide into a little drawing (a star, a heart, a fish…) and it fills with colour.

Solve a level in as many moves as it has dots (or fewer) for three stars. Undo takes back the last move, Restart starts the knot again, and Hint (three per knot) moves one dot to a better place. The lessons can be watched again in Settings › How to play.

Daily knot and leaderboards

The daily knot is the same for everyone and changes at midnight UTC. The leaderboard has three tabs: Today (fewest moves on the daily knot), All time (total stars from the levels) and Mine (your own daily solves). Your name is a random one picked by the game; tap New name to get another. No account is needed.

Settings
