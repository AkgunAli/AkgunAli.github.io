Privacy Policy

Knot: Untangle Doodles · Last updated 27 November 2026

Knot: Untangle Doodles is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads and no third-party analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Leaderboard

To show the leaderboards, the game creates a random identifier and a random secret on your device the first time it needs them. When you solve a level or the daily knot, it sends that identifier with the solve (which puzzle, where you moved each dot, and the result) to our server, which checks it and stores it with a random name such as "Lucky Otter 42". You cannot type a name or any other text. The identifier is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or device, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. The server is a Supabase project; the data is used only for the leaderboards.

To have your scores removed, open a request (see Contact) with the name shown in the game. Deleting the app deletes the identifier from your device.

On your device

Your stars, best moves, daily streak and settings (language, sound, vibration) are stored only on your device.

Children

The game does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes (for example if ads are added), we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
