Orbit: Comet Jump

Tap. Jump. Stay in orbit.

How to play

A little comet circles the sun on one of two rings. Tap anywhere on the screen and it jumps to the other ring. Red arcs end the run; blinking arcs come and go. Stars give points, and stars collected in a row make combos up to ×5. Every lap around the sun counts, and laps 10, 25, 50 and 100 give a bonus.

Now and then you will see a golden star (50 points), a shield (forgives one crash) or a clock (a few seconds of slow motion). Leaving a ring just before a red arc gives a small "Close!" bonus. Once per run you can continue after a crash. The lessons can be watched again in Settings › How to play.

Daily run and leaderboards

The daily run gives everyone the same sky and changes at midnight UTC. The leaderboard has three tabs: Today (the daily run), All time (best endless score) and Mine (your own best runs). Your name is a random one picked by the game; tap New name to get another. No account is needed.

Settings

  • Language: the menus come in 70 languages.
  • Sound and Vibration can be turned off.
  • Comet: new comet colours unlock as you collect stars.

Contact

Found a problem? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed; start the title with "Orbit"). Please include your device, iOS version, language and the version shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.
