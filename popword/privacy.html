Privacy Policy

Popword · Last updated 18 November 2026

Popword is a word puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server, and it does not track you across apps or websites. The game does not send any data off your device.

On your device

Your progress (levels, stars, daily streak) and settings (language, language level, categories, difficulty, sound, vibration) are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

The game does not ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this changes (for example if leaderboards or ads are added), we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
