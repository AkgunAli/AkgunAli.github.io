Popword

Spell a word. Watch the rest fall.

How to play

Slide your finger across touching letter tiles (sideways and diagonally too) to spell a word. The tiles pop and everything above them falls. Empty the box in the target number of words for three stars. Stuck? Tap Hint to see the next word, or Undo to take a word back.

Daily puzzles

The daily puzzle and the word of the day are the same for everyone and change at midnight UTC. The word of the day is one hidden word: use every tile to find it.

Settings

  • Language: words and menus come in 10 languages.
  • Language level: learners get shorter words.
  • Categories: choose which kinds of words your levels use.
  • Difficulty: Easy marks every first letter, Medium shows how long each word is, Hard gives no help.

Contact

Found a problem or a word that should be accepted? Open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed; start the title with "Popword"). Please include your device, iOS version, language and the version shown at the bottom of Settings in the app.
