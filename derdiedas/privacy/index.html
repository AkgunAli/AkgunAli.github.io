Der Die Das mit Ali Abi

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 14, 2026

Summary

Der Die Das mit Ali Abi does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your favorites, recent searches, game scores, statistics (such as words you got right and your daily streak), language, theme and notification settings are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Translations

Translations are created on your device with Apple's Translation framework. If you tap "Open in Google Translate", the word is opened in your browser and Google's privacy policy applies there.

Friend duel (Game Center)

The optional friend duel uses Apple Game Center to connect two players. Apple processes your Game Center profile and match data under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive or store this data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (word of the day, quiz) are scheduled locally on your device. No push server is used.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.
